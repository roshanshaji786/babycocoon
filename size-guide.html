How to measure your baby

1

Length / height

Lay your baby flat on a mat, hold a soft tape from head to heel. Round up to the nearest cm.

2

Weight

Weigh after a feed, before a nappy change, for the most accurate number.

3

Pick by age + weight

Use the chart below. If your baby is between sizes, choose the larger size for comfort.
